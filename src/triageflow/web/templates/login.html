Sort patients by how urgently they need care — in seconds, with the reason shown.

A nurse enters the complaint and vital signs. TriageFlow calculates a priority from the South African Triage Scale (SATS) — the same chart used at the desk — and a language model reads the complaint to suggest signs the numbers alone can’t show. The nurse decides; the system only proposes. Nothing joins the queue until a named clinician confirms it.
